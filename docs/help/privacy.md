# Privacy

Better Screenshot sends a screenshot to a third-party service **only when you click \[Upload]** (or after every F2 if you enabled `autoUpload`).

| What | Where | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The screenshot you chose | [catbox.moe](https://catbox.moe) or [Imgur](https://imgur.com), depending on `host` | When you click **\[Upload]** |

* Uploaded images are **public to anyone who has the link**.
* They are subject to the host's own terms: [catbox.moe FAQ](https://catbox.moe/faq.php), [Imgur terms](https://imgur.com/tos).
* Nothing else is collected or sent: no analytics, no usage data, no account information.
* **\[Copy]** never sends anything online: the image stays on your computer.
